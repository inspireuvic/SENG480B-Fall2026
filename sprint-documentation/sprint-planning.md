# AI as a Team Member

Generative and agentic AI can contribute to your project, but **your team remains responsible for the decisions, outputs, and impacts of the work produced**.

Your goal is not to use AI for everything. Your goal is to make deliberate decisions about **where AI adds value, what responsibilities it should have, and where human judgment is necessary**.

## 6. How Much Autonomy Will AI Have?

For your major AI-supported tasks, decide how much independence AI should have.

### Level 0: Human Only

The task is completed without AI.

### Level 1: Advisor

AI provides ideas, explanations, critiques, recommendations, or alternatives. Humans perform the work and make the decisions.

### Level 2: Collaborator

Humans and AI iteratively work together to produce an artifact or solve a problem.

### Level 3: Delegate

AI produces a substantial artifact or completes a defined task. A human reviews and decides whether to use the result.

### Level 4: Agent

AI independently performs a sequence of actions or subtasks toward a goal, with defined boundaries, checkpoints, and human oversight.
